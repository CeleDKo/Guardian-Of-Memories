Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Guardian of Memories collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Who operates the app

Guardian of Memories is an application intended to help users create, save, organize, and revisit personal memories. The application is maintained by its developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Guardian of Memories processes the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your name and email address received through Google Sign-In, and the account identifier used by Firebase Authentication to associate your app data with your account.
  • Memory content: the text and other information you enter into the app as memory entries. The app stores this content in the Firebase database configured for the project.
  • Basic technical and service information: information that Google, Firebase, or the platform may process to provide authentication, database hosting, security, reliability, and service diagnostics. The exact technical information depends on the Firebase products and SDK configuration enabled in the released app.

We do not ask for your Google password. Google handles the authentication process; the app receives the authentication result and the basic profile information permitted by the sign-in configuration.

3. Google Sign-In

The app uses Google Sign-In to let you authenticate with a Google account. It uses the basic identity information made available for sign-in, such as your name, email address, and Google account identifier, to establish and recognize your app account.

The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, Google Contacts, or other Google user data through additional Google API scopes as part of the sign-in flow described here. If the app's permissions or integrations change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes take effect.

Google processes information in accordance with its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. How we use information

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • to authenticate you and maintain your app account;
  • to associate your memory entries with the correct account;
  • to save, retrieve, and display the memories you create;
  • to maintain the security, availability, and correct operation of the application; and
  • to respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not use Google API data for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

5. Firebase and service providers

The app uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and a Firebase database, to support sign-in and store account-related data and memory entries. Google provides and operates Firebase infrastructure. Information processed by Firebase is subject to Firebase's privacy and security information and Google's applicable terms.

These service providers process information on behalf of the app to provide the services described in this policy. We do not authorize them to use your memory content for their own advertising purposes.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Your account information and memory entries are stored using the Firebase project configured for the application. We use the security controls available through the services and the app's configuration to protect stored information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Information is retained while it is needed to provide the app and maintain your account. If you request account or data deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information associated with your account, subject to technical limitations, legitimate operational needs, and any legal obligations. Backup copies may persist for a limited period as part of the service provider's normal backup and recovery processes.

Before publishing: configure Firebase Database Security Rules so each user can access only the data they are authorized to access. Do not rely on this policy as a substitute for access-control rules.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by Google/Firebase as described above, or disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority. We may also need to use or disclose limited information to protect the security and integrity of the app, its users, or the public.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In and whether to create memory entries. You may stop using the app at any time.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account, email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the email address associated with your account and include “Guardian of Memories — Privacy Request” in the subject. Do not send your password. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account before acting on a request.

Signing out of the app does not, by itself, delete your account or memory entries. If an in-app account deletion feature is added, its operation will be described here.

9. Children's privacy

Guardian of Memories is not designed specifically for children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the details below so we can review the request.

10. International processing

Google/Firebase may process and store information in data centers located in countries other than the country where you live. The location and applicable safeguards depend on the Firebase project configuration and Google's infrastructure. Consult the Firebase and Google privacy documentation linked above for further information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
