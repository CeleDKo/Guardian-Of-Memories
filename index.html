02 / ACCOUNT

Sign in securely.

The app offers Google Sign-In so you can authenticate with your Google account. During sign-in, the app requests basic account information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, and profile image if provided and used by the app.

Firebase services are used to support account-related functionality and store app data, including memory entries, in the Firebase database configured for the project.
